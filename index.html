Xinyue Zeng (Susan)

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Virginia Tech, advised by Prof. Dawei Zhou. My research focuses on self-improving AI agents and reliable, efficient LLM reasoning, with an emphasis on learning from experience, RL post-training, and test-time scaling. I am also exploring recursive self-improvement (RSI), particularly how agents can improve the way they learn and adapt.

In Summer 2026, I was invited to join Microsoft’s Frontier Tuning AI team as a research intern, extending my research on test-time inference and hallucination control to long-horizon AI agents. My statistical training spans a B.S. in Statistics from Zhejiang University and an M.S. in Biostatistics from UNC–Chapel Hill.
